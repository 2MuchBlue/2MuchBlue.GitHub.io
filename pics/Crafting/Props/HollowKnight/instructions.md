# 2MuchBlue's Hornet Plush Instructions!
This is an overview of how to actually put together a lovely little Hornet plush from the game Hollow Knight / Silksong! Most of these instructions are suggestions and just things that I found usually work.

There are more resources in the "Resources" section on the bottom of this page!

---
## Preparations
None of these pattern prices have any seam allowance! Please account for this and add however much you feel you might need ( I tend to use 1 cm-ish of space )!
On the eyes, you don't need any allowance. On the cape, the outer round edge and the neck hole don't need any ( I don't hem it, and its lovely ) either. The cape should have some allowance on the straight edges.

DON'T CUT THE HORNS OUT BEFORE YOU SEW THE HEAD TOGETHER! It will just make your life harder! Just cut out the circle, attach eyes, and after you've traced the horned outline to sew the head panels together, *then* you can trim whatever, otherwise its a pain to sew around them.

For materials, I would recommend anything that doesn't fray, is soft, and you like it! I tend to use fleeces because they are soft don't fray. They also have a little stretch so the plushes can be stuffed more full.

If you plan on satin stitching the eyes on, I recommend a little interfacing on the backside of the head to support the eyes, experience with past projects tell me its much easier to satin stitch with support behind it. If you don't want to satin stitch, I found that it is totally possible to just hand sew the eyes on after the head is stuffed.

These instructions are just guidelines! Take nothing as absolute! This is my fist time doing something like this so there is likely an easier way I don't know about! 

---
## Instructions
1) Put both eyes on one of the head panels ( this will be Hornet's front face ) around where they appear on the head panel pattern. I'd recommend tacking each eye down, then using a satin stitch around the border with interfacing on the back to make it look nice! ( look up "applique embroidery" if you need help! )

2) Sew the two (2) head panels together *right sides together* with a little gap somewhere ( usually the neck ) so you can turn it inside-out. Make sure you go around the contour of the horns. Make sure they do not connect otherwise you won't be able to turn it out. If you want the horns together, after the head is turned out and stuffed, you can sew the insides of the horns together with a ladder stitch or something.

3) Stuff the head! Once stuffed to satisfaction, sew up the remaining gap with a ladder stitch.

4) Sew 2 (too) body panels together *right sides together* with a little gap, again, to be able to turn it inside-out.

5) Stuff the Body! Again, once the body is stuffed to satisfaction, sew up the remaining gap with a ladder stitch.

6) Attatch the head to the body ( I use a lot of ladder stitches by hand, but most anything will work )

7) Wrap the cape around Hornet and with the little peice of seam allowance on the edges, sew it up! ( I tack it down, then come back with the machine and a straight stitch. )

8) Celebrate! You have finished! Take pictures! Annoy your friends! Have fun!
